# Fictional example: Fieldnote workshop waitlist
Entirely fictional practice brief. All business facts below are scenario assumptions, not real-world evidence or results.

## Intake
Fieldnote is an imagined workshop organizer offering a draft “Plan your first community workshop” session. Audience: first-time volunteer organizers. Objective: learn whether this topic attracts qualified interest. Scenario source F1: this example's invented brief, 2026-10-16. Resources: one organizer, three hours/week for two weeks; existing website and brand social account. Budget: $0 planned. No audience size or baseline provided.

## Plan
Offer: register interest in a workshop whose date and price are not set. Primary event: a unique valid interest submission that indicates the person is organizing their first workshop. Proposed learning target: five such submissions by the end of week two; this is an arbitrary starting target for discussion, not a forecast.

Use the website as owned destination and one brand social post as a rented discovery channel. Audience fit is a hypothesis awaiting prior engagement evidence. Omit paid ads because budget is zero. Defer partner channels because no partner has agreed. Publisher: brand only; founder and partner posts are unnecessary for this scope.

| Due | Task | Owner | Effort | Dependency | Acceptance |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Week 1 | Confirm topic, audience, and consent wording | Organizer | 0.5h | None | Accurate interest-only offer |
| Week 1 | Draft and review interest page | Organizer | 1h | Confirmed offer | Clear unknown date/price; no payment |
| Week 1 | Prepare post and review destination | Organizer | 0.5h | Page draft | Link works before any launch |
| Week 2 | Authorized manual publication and response handling | Organizer, proposed | 1h | Approval and functioning form | Human approval; consent reviewed |
| Week 2 | Review anonymized count and topic feedback | Organizer | 1h | Actual submissions | Distinguish qualified/total; no invented results |

Total planned effort 4h within 6h capacity; 2h buffer. No task above has actually been executed.

## Representative asset
Brand post draft: “Planning your first community workshop? We're shaping a session on choosing a topic, outlining the agenda, and preparing the room. Register your interest to hear when the date and price are confirmed.” CTA: Register interest. Destination: missing; publication blocked until approved URL exists. Reviewer: organizer.

## Measurement
Manually count consented, qualified interest submissions once at week two. Baseline: unknown. Do not infer reach-to-interest conversion without a reliable denominator, or claim social attribution from total signups. If fewer than five qualified submissions, inspect whether the page was reached and whether the offer was understood before changing the topic. If five or more, ask the organizer to decide whether to schedule; no automatic launch or spending.

## Blockers
Confirm destination, form privacy/consent, actual audience access, owner, date horizon, and whether the proposed target is useful. Draft only. No observed campaign results.
