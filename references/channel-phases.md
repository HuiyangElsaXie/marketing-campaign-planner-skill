# Small-team channel and phase selection
Adapted narrowly from the upstream launch module identified in THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md.

- Owned: existing website, permissioned email list, or maintained community. Use an available destination that can receive the requested action. “Owned” does not imply freedom from service rules or consent requirements.
- Rented: a social platform or marketplace with relevant audience evidence. Consider policy and algorithm dependence; use one feasible surface before multiplying assets.
- Borrowed: a partner's audience, guest appearance, or collaborator. Treat participation as unconfirmed until agreed. Drafting a pitch does not authorize sending it.

Map the path from attention to one useful action. Choose channels by audience fit, access, effort, and evidence, not by the assumption that every launch needs every channel.

Compress upstream launch phasing into preparation, release-ready assets, and learning. Preparation covers product truth, destination, assets, support, and measurement. Release-ready means approved material waiting for authorized human execution. Learning means a proposed review of observed signals; do not fabricate a completed launch or its results.

Small update: one targeted asset may suffice. Larger release: add assets only when owners and dependencies are clear. Numerical targets are planning inputs, never guaranteed outcomes.
